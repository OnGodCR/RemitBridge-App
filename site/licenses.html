Open-source licenses

Last updated October 17, 2026

RemitBridge is built on work that others share. This page lists the open-source software in the app, and the fonts and images in the app and on this site, with their licenses. Thank you to everyone who made them.

Fonts

  • Plus Jakarta Sans, copyright 2020 The Plus Jakarta Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/tokotype/PlusJakartaSans), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  • Hind, copyright 2014 Indian Type Foundry (info@indiantypefoundry.com), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Images and icons

  • Country flags come from flagcdn.com, in the public domain.
  • This site's feature icons are from Lucide, under the ISC License.
  • The app's own drawings, the bridge mark and the screenshots are RemitBridge Labs' own work.
  • Google's "G" and the Sign in with Apple button are used as Google's and Apple's sign-in guidelines allow; the marks belong to them.

Software from Google and Apple

The app uses Google's ML Kit (to read receipt text on the phone), Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging (for notifications on Android), and Apple's own frameworks, under Google's and Apple's terms for those kits.
