RemitBridge

Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026

RemitBridge is a free app that compares the cost of sending money abroad. It is run by Angad Kochar as a personal project. This policy covers the RemitBridge mobile app and this site. In short: comparing prices needs no account, we don't sell data, and there is no advertising or third-party tracking.

Without an account

You can compare prices without signing up. The app still records how it's used, to count users and see which features help. Each record holds:

  • a random ID created when the app is first opened, which is not linked to your name, device or advertising ID;
  • what happened, such as "comparison run", with the choices involved, such as the route and the amount you checked;
  • whether you're on iOS or Android, the app version, and the time.

We don't collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

With an account

An account is optional. If you create one, we also store:

  • your email address, or the account details Apple or Google share if you sign in with them;
  • anything you choose to save, such as comparisons and transfers you enter yourself (provider, amounts, rate, date and any note);
  • the usage records above, linked to your account while you're signed in.

Saved items are private to your account. Database rules stop anyone else from reading them.

Scanning a receipt

The app asks for camera or photo access only when you scan a transfer receipt, and uses only the photo you take or choose. It reads the receipt on your phone first. Before anything leaves your phone, it covers names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and card, account and reference numbers with solid boxes, removes the photo's hidden details (such as where it was taken), and shows you exactly what would be sent. You can cover more before sending.

What's sent is the covered photo and the text read from it. Our server checks the text again and covers anything the phone missed; if it finds anything, it doesn't use or keep the photo. What we keep is the values read (such as the amounts, fee, tax, exchange rate, currencies, date, provider and how you paid), where each came from, and when. We don't keep the text, and we keep the photo only if you choose to. A scanned receipt carries no account, phone number or device ID.

On WhatsApp, if you send a photo of a receipt, our server reads it and covers the same personal details before the values are read; only the covered text goes on to be read, never the photo. The photo is deleted once it's read and is never stored. To send you the answer a few minutes later, we keep your number encrypted until the answer is sent, and never longer than an hour.

A scanned receipt is deleted after 30 days, and you can delete it sooner in the app. If you turn on "Keep this receipt for research", we keep the covered photo and the values, to study what people really pay, and report them only as totals.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: signing you in and keeping what you save.
  • To measure and improve it: counting users, comparisons and features used. We only report these as totals, never about an individual.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in, in the United States.
  • Resend sends the sign-in code email, if you sign in with email.
  • Apple or Google handle sign-in if you choose them.
  • OpenRouter passes a scanned receipt to an AI model (currently Alibaba's Qwen) when the app can't read a value on its own. It gets only the covered photo and text, never the original, and we ask it to use only model hosts that don't store or train on what they're sent.

To check a receipt, our server also asks Frankfurter, a public exchange-rate service, for the rate on the receipt's date. That request holds only the two currencies and the date.

Each processes data only to provide its service to RemitBridge.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything saved to it at once. Usage records made while you were signed in are kept only as anonymous totals: the link to your account is removed. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

RemitBridge is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
