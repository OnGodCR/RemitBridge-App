## What to ask for

Keep the first message to a conversation, not a commitment:

- which countries and ways of sending matter most to the people they work with;
- whether a flyer, a short workshop, or a plain link would help (D48: partners get a
  link like `app.remitbridgelabs.org/p/<name>`, which counts visits without tracking anyone);
- for Horn of Africa Services: how families send to Somalia and Eritrea today, and
  whether they'd help source prices by hand (PRD 6.4).

Things not to say (D49): RemitBridge is not a nonprofit, charity or 501(c)(3). "Free" and
"non-commercial" are fine.

## Draft email

**Subject:** A free tool for families sending money home

```
Hi {Organization} team,

My name is Angad Kochar, and I'm building RemitBridge, a free app that shows people in the US what it really costs to send money to family abroad, and which provider leaves their family the most. It looks at the fee and the exchange rate together, since a lot of the cost hides in the rate. There are no ads or affiliate links, nobody pays to be listed, and the app never handles anyone's money.

I'm writing because many {community} families in the Seattle area send money home, and I'd like to build this with the people who would use it. We're starting with India, Mexico, the Philippines and Vietnam, and anyone can ask for another country in the app. Those requests decide what we add next.

Would you be open to a short conversation? I'd like to hear which countries and ways of sending matter most to the people you work with, and whether a flyer, a workshop or a simple link would be useful. There's no cost and nothing to sign.

Thanks for reading, and I'm happy to answer any questions.

Best,
Angad Kochar
RemitBridge
remitbridgesystemlabs@gmail.com
https://app.remitbridgelabs.org
```

The {community} word for each: Filipino (Filipino Community of Seattle), Indian American
(IACS), Latino (Casa Latina, El Centro de la Raza), Vietnamese (Kandelia).

For Horn of Africa Services and East African Community Services, replace the second
paragraph with:

```
I'm writing because Somalia and Eritrea are routes we can't handle like the others. Very few providers publish their prices online, so the only honest way to show them is with help from people who know how families actually send money there. I'd like to learn how the families you work with send today, so that if we cover these routes, the prices we show are the ones people really pay.
```

For the Financial Empowerment Network:

```
I'm writing because the coaches in your network meet people who send money to family abroad, and the cost of those transfers is easy to miss, since much of it hides in the exchange rate. If it would help, I'd be glad to walk your coaches through the app or share a simple link they can pass on.
```

For the Office of Immigrant and Refugee Affairs:

```
I'm writing because your office connects immigrant and refugee communities with resources, and sending money home is part of life for many of them. If a free tool like this belongs on your resource list, I'd be glad to share anything you need to check it first.
```

El Centro de la Raza takes messages through its web form, so paste the standard text there.
