Where do the prices come from?

From four kinds of source:

  • providers' public websites;
  • provider APIs we use with the provider's permission, such as Wise's;
  • prices entered by RemitBridge staff from a provider's public price calculator;
  • mid-market exchange rates from Frankfurter, a public exchange-rate service.

Each price is checked against the mid-market rate before it's shown, and shows when it was checked. A price that fails a check is held back. Prices change during the day, so one may be out of date by the time you see it.

Can I send money through RemitBridge?

No. RemitBridge only shows prices. It never sends, holds or exchanges money. To send, go to the provider you pick.

How are providers ranked?

By the amount your family receives, and nothing else. No provider pays us, and there are no affiliate links.

Why does a price differ from what I see on the provider's site?

Rates move during the day, and some fees change with the amount or how you pay. The app shows when each price was checked. If one looks wrong, email us with the provider, route and amount.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account, then Delete account. Everything saved to your account is deleted at once. See the privacy policy.
