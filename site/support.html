Where do the prices come from?

From four kinds of source:

  • providers' public websites;
  • provider APIs we use with the provider's permission, such as Wise's;
  • prices entered by RemitBridge staff from a provider's public price calculator;
  • mid-market references such as Frankfurter, ExchangeRate-API and Wise.

Each price is checked against the mid-market rate before it's shown, and its details show when it was collected. A price that fails a check is held back. Prices change during the day, so one may be out of date by the time you see it.

Can I send money through RemitBridge?

No. RemitBridge only shows prices. It never sends, holds or exchanges money. To send, go to the provider you pick.

How are providers ranked?

By the amount your family receives, and nothing else. No provider pays us, and there are no affiliate links.

Why does a price differ from what I see on the provider's site?

Rates move during the day, and some fees change with the amount or how you pay. Tap a price to see when it was collected. If one looks wrong, email us with the provider, route and amount.

What can Remmy do?

Ask Remmy to explain fees, compare a route, open account settings or start a lesson. A requested navigation happens once. Reopening Remmy keeps the conversation without repeating the action. Start a new chat to clear the conversation from app memory. Saving a comparison needs your tap and an account. Remmy cannot send money or change your settings.

Chat uses Cloudflare or Groq after you allow it. Do not share passwords, codes or bank details. AI can be wrong: confirm prices before acting. To flag an answer, tap Report reply beneath it and confirm inside the app. The selected reply is kept for review separately from the chat. For a connection error, retry once you are online; daily and short-term limits protect the free service. Compare and Learn remain available when AI capacity is unavailable.

Why bank-account funding, and what is RemitBridge choice?

Compare uses bank-account-funded quotes only. RemitBridge choice marks the highest amount received among the listed standard quotes for your choices; promotions are separate. Tap a quote for its source and collection time. Hand-checked prices can remain eligible for up to seven days. Prices can change sooner, so confirm on the provider's site.

Learning, alerts and newsletter

Learn includes lessons and games, with progress on your phone. Rate alerts need an account and notification permission, and send at most once per alert per day. The newsletter is optional: turn it off in Account or use the unsubscribe link in any issue.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account, then Delete account. Everything saved to your account is deleted at once. See the privacy policy.
