Account Holder:

1. Go to https://developer.apple.com/contact/app-store/ and sign in.
2. Choose **App Review**, then **Inquire about the status of my app review** (if that option
   isn't shown, use **Request an expedited app review**; the same text works there).
3. Pick RemitBridge from the app list, paste the message below, and send.

Replies arrive at the account's email and in App Store Connect (App Review, the submission's
messages), so check both.
